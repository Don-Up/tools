### 面试黄金法则：STAR 原则话术模板

在介绍项目经历时，不要罗列技术名词，要用 **STAR 原则** 讲故事。以下是你需要的关键连接词：

- **S** (Situation) — **“The project was...”**
  - 描述项目背景（规模、团队、周期）
- **T** (Task) — **“My responsibility was to...”**
  - 描述你个人的任务和目标
- **A** (Action) — **“I designed/implemented/refactored...”**
  - 详细描述你“怎么做”的（用上面的动词）
- **R** (Result) — **“As a result, we achieved...”**
  - 突出你的行动带来的量化成果（用上面的量化词）

**STAR 完整示例（后端面试）：**

> *“**S**：We were migrating a legacy monolithic payment system to microservices. **T**：My responsibility was to design the new order service and ensure data consistency. **A**：I designed the **database schema** using PostgreSQL, implemented **event-driven communication** with Kafka, and introduced a **saga pattern** to handle distributed transactions. **R**：As a result, we reduced the payment processing latency by 40% and improved system availability to 99.99%.”*

### 面试官常问的高频句式回应

当你没听清或需要思考时：

- *“Let me rephrase that...”*（让我换个说法...）
- *“To give you some context...”*（先给你一些背景...）
- *“I’d like to highlight that...”*（我想强调一点...）
- *“In my experience...”*（根据我的经验...）
- *“From a technical perspective...”*（从技术角度来看...）
- *“That’s a great question. Let me think...”*（好问题，我想一下...）
