Coloring rules

Words separated by commas. Changes apply instantly. Later rules win for the same word. Tick "ends with" to match by suffix (e.g. ly, 's). The apostrophe counts as a normal letter, so 's matches there's. Tick "hide" to make matched words invisible while they keep their space — hidden words show up again while the video is paused.

Example:
I am the four of them
