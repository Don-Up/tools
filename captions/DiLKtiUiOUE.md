This is a touching and sad story. A man worked at a daycare to ease his grief over his late daughter, yet he was misunderstood and finally fired unfairly.
这是一个令人心酸的故事。一位男子为缅怀逝去的女儿来到托儿所工作，却遭到他人误解，最终还被无辜辞退。

When the bell rang at the end of my first day working at the daycare, I felt incredibly **proud of**(为……感到骄傲) myself.
> 在托儿所工作的第一天结束，铃声响起时，我心里满是自豪。

But when the parents **lined up**(排队) to pick up their kids, one of the mothers saw me and immediately **marched over**(大步走过来).
> 可当家长们排队接孩子时，一位母亲看到了我，立刻朝我走了过来。

She loudly **demanded to know**(追问) why a grown man would want to work around children.
> 她大声地质问，一个成年男人为什么要在孩子身边工作。

The other parents turned and I felt a **hitch in my throat**(喉咙哽咽，百感交集), but I stayed calm and told her the truth.
> 其他家长纷纷侧目，我顿时喉咙发紧，但还是保持冷静，道出了实情。

My daughter **passed away**(去世), ma'am. She would have been the same age as the kids in this class.
> 女士，我的女儿已经离世了。她本该和班里这些孩子一般大。

Being around them helps me feel close to her.
> 待在孩子们身边，能让我感觉离女儿更近一些。

I expected her to understand, but her face didn't **soften**(缓和，变得温和) and she jabbed her finger in my chest.
> 我本以为她会理解我，可她神色丝毫未变，还用手指戳着我的胸口。

Well, that's even weirder, using innocent children as **grief support**(精神慰藉).
> 呵，那就更怪异了，居然拿无辜的孩子来排解悲伤。

My **blood ran cold**(心里一凉，不寒而栗) as everyone around me stared, and I tried not to let it affect me.
> 周围所有人都盯着我，我心里一阵发凉，努力不让自己受到影响。

But the next day, the same mom **stormed in**(怒气冲冲地闯进来) while I was mid-lesson.
> 但第二天，我正在上课，这位母亲又怒气冲冲地闯了进来。

Her husband stood behind her in his cop uniform, and they marched to my desk.
> 她的丈夫身着警服站在她身后，两人径直走到我的桌前。

He demanded I step away so he could search my computer.
> 他勒令我站到一旁，要检查我的电脑。

He began **flicking through**(快速浏览) it, and one of the kids asked me, "Why are they searching your computer?"
> 他开始快速翻看电脑，有个孩子问我：“他们为什么要检查你的电脑呀？”

The woman answered, "Because he might be a bad man."
> 那位女士答道：“因为他可能是个坏人。”

I felt my legs **buckling**(发软，站立不稳) and when I looked over, the daycare owner was in the doorway.
> 我只觉得双腿发软，转头看去，托儿所的负责人正站在门口。

I stood **frozen**(僵住，一动不动) as the cop went through my entire laptop, scrolling pictures of my dead daughter.
> 警察翻遍了我的整台笔记本电脑，浏览着我已故女儿的照片，我呆立在原地。

He was clearly looking for something **incriminating**(能证明有罪的证据).
> 他显然是想找出一些能指控我的证据。

After finding nothing, he **slammed**(猛地合上) my laptop shut so hard I heard a crack.
> 一无所获后，他狠狠合上笔记本电脑，我都听到了碎裂的声响。

"A parent is uncomfortable with you being here. I know you didn't do anything wrong, but cops getting involved is a terrible look for the daycare. I'm going to have to **let you go**(辞退你)."
> “有家长对你留在这儿心存顾虑。我知道你并无过错，但警方介入会让托儿所名声受损，我只能辞退你了。”

I was in **disbelief**(难以置信) and stood up with shaking legs.
> 我难以置信，双腿颤抖着站起身。

I packed my things and placed the framed photo of my daughter in the box.
> 我收拾好个人物品，将女儿的相框放进箱子里。

And as I walked through the lobby, I saw the same mom there. She was **smirking**(得意地冷笑) with her arms crossed.
> 当我走过大厅时，又看到了那位母亲。她双臂交叉，面露冷笑。
