This warm story tells of a boy who wanted to quit swimming out of fear of disappointing his father. In the end, he learned that his dad cherished their time together far more than swimming itself.
这是一则暖心故事。男孩因害怕让父亲失望，一直不敢提出放弃游泳，最后他才明白，比起游泳，父亲更珍惜和他相伴的时光。

I sat in the truck with my hand on the door trying to **figure out**(想清楚，盘算) how to tell my dad I wanted to quit swimming.
> 我坐在卡车里，手搭在车门上，纠结着该怎么跟爸爸说我不想再游泳了。

He turned the engine off and reached into the bag between us. "Eat up. We've got a few minutes," he said and put a breakfast sandwich in my hand.
> 他关掉引擎，伸手拿过我俩中间的袋子。“快吃吧，还有点时间。”他说着，把一份早餐三明治递到我手里。

"You know, I stayed in the 100 fly at your age," he nodded toward the building. "42 seconds. My name's still on the wall in there."
> “你知道吗，我像你这么大的时候也参加百米蝶泳。”他朝游泳馆的方向点了点头，“成绩42秒，我的名字至今还留在里面的荣誉墙上。”

He'd driven me to this pool at 5 every morning since I was 6. And he said the same thing every drive that these mornings were the best part of his day.
> 从我六岁起，他每天早上五点都会开车送我来泳池。每次路上他都会说，清晨这段时光是他一天中最美好的时刻。

And here I was about to take them away from him. The words were sitting right there in my throat. But when I opened my mouth, nothing came out.
> 而我现在，却要夺走这份美好。话就在嘴边，可我张了张嘴，终究没能说出口。

I just held the sandwich, still wrapped the way I'd held everything for months, my hands smelling like chlorine I couldn't scrub off.
> 我就那样握着还没拆开的三明治，数月以来我一直把心事藏在心底，手上满是怎么也洗不掉的泳池消毒水气味。

"You're quiet," he said, nervous about **regionals**(地区选拔赛). "Yeah," I said, and stared out the window.
> “你怎么闷闷不乐的，是担心地区选拔赛吗？”他问道。“嗯。”我应了一声，望向窗外。

We walked in together and he climbed up to his usual spot in the **bleachers**(看台). Coach lined us up for the 100 fly, my dad's old race.
> 我们一起走进场馆，他走上看台常坐的位置。教练让我们列队准备百米蝶泳，这也是父亲曾经参赛的项目。

And out of the corner of my eye, I caught him leaning forward with his elbows on his knees.
> 我用余光瞥见他身体前倾，双肘撑在膝盖上。

I pushed off the wall soft and barely kicked. And by the first turn, the kid in the next lane was already half a body ahead of me.
> 我轻轻蹬离池壁，几乎没有用力蹬水。第一个折返过后，隔壁泳道的选手已经领先我半个身子。

"What are you doing out there? Move," coach yelled. But I didn't go any faster.
> “你在干什么？加快速度！”教练大声呵斥，可我依旧没有提速。

When I came up for air, I caught my dad in the stands, and he wasn't watching the clock or the other swimmers. He was watching me.
> 我抬头换气时，看向看台上的父亲。他没有盯着计时钟，也没有关注其他选手，目光一直落在我身上。

He looked like he was starting to realize something was wrong. I hit the wall last and instead of pushing off for the next lap, I just hung there on the lane rope.
> 他似乎察觉到了不对劲。我最后一个触壁，没有继续蹬水游下一圈，只是抓着分道绳停在了水里。

Coach blew his whistle and up in the stands, my dad was already on his feet.
> 教练吹响了哨子，而看台上的父亲也猛地站了起来。

Afterwards, we sat in the truck and he didn't start the engine. He handed me my water and watched me not drink it.
> 比赛结束后，我们回到车里，他没有发动车子。他递给我一瓶水，看着我迟迟没有喝。

"Why do you look like that?" He asked. I set the bottle down in my lap. "I don't want to swim anymore, Dad. I haven't for a long time."
> “你怎么这副样子？”他问道。我把水瓶放在腿上：“爸爸，我不想再游泳了，其实我早就不想坚持了。”

He got quiet and stared out the windshield. "Is that what's been **eating you**(让你烦心)?"
> 他沉默下来，望着挡风玻璃外。“原来这就是一直困扰你的事？”

I nodded and the rest came out in a rush. "I didn't want to let you down. I know how much this means to you."
> 我点了点头，一股脑说出了心里话：“我不想让你失望，我知道游泳对你来说意义重大。”

He laughed, not in a mean way. "Buddy, I don't care about the swimming. I never did."
> 他温和地笑了起来：“孩子，我从来都不在意游泳本身。”

He put the truck in reverse, then put it back in park. "You think I get up at 5 for the ribbons? I do it for the drives, the sandwiches, 20 quiet minutes with you before the world's even awake."
> 他先是挂了倒挡，随后又切回驻车挡。“你以为我每天五点起床，是为了那些奖牌吗？我珍惜的是这段车程、这份早餐，是天亮前和你独处的这二十分钟。”

He turned to me. "We can quit the team tomorrow and keep the mornings."
> 他转头看向我：“我们明天就退出游泳队，但清晨的时光，我们照旧一起度过。”

I picked up my sandwich and finally unwrapped it. And I never swam another race after that morning.
> 我拿起三明治，终于拆开了包装。从那天起，我再也没有参加过游泳比赛。
