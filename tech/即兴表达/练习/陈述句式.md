以下是为每种句式提供的五组中英对照例句（英文放在中文下方的引用块中）：

---

### 1. with 结构

**中文例句：**  
1. Python 是一种高级编程语言，具有简洁的语法和强大的库支持。  
2. 这款智能手机配备 6.7 英寸屏幕和 5000mAh 电池。  
3. 这份报告包含详细的数据分析和图表说明。  
4. 她穿着一件白色连衣裙，带着一顶草帽。

> **English:**  
> 1. Python is a high-level programming language with concise syntax and powerful library support.  
> 2. This smartphone comes with a 6.7-inch screen and a 5000mAh battery.  
> 3. This report includes detailed data analysis with chart illustrations.  
> 4. She wore a white dress with a straw hat.

---

### 2. 分句拆分

**中文例句：**  
1. 机器学习模型需要大量数据。它们通过反复训练来优化参数。  
2. 这个项目时间紧迫。我们必须在两周内完成原型。  
3. 城市交通日益拥堵。政府正在推广公共交通系统。  
4. 这本书非常畅销。它已经被翻译成三十多种语言。  
5. 他每天早起锻炼。这让他整天精力充沛。

> **English:**  
> 1. Machine learning models require large amounts of data. They optimize parameters through iterative training.  
> 2. This project has a tight schedule. We must finish the prototype within two weeks.  
> 3. Urban traffic is getting increasingly congested. The government is promoting public transit systems.  
> 4. This book is a bestseller. It has been translated into over 30 languages.  
> 5. He works out early every morning. This keeps him energetic throughout the day.

---

### 3. 并列谓语

**中文例句：**  
1. 她走进教室，放下书包，然后开始讲课。  
2. 我们需要收集需求，设计方案，并编写测试用例。  
3. 这家公司研发芯片，生产设备，并提供售后服务。  
4. 他打开电脑，登录系统，并检查了所有邮件。  
5. 春天来了，花朵绽放，鸟儿开始筑巢。

> **English:**  
> 1. She walked into the classroom, put down her bag, and started the lecture.  
> 2. We need to gather requirements, design solutions, and write test cases.  
> 3. This company develops chips, manufactures equipment, and provides after-sales support.  
> 4. He turned on the computer, logged into the system, and checked all the emails.  
> 5. Spring arrives, flowers bloom, and birds begin to build nests.

---

### 4. 双谓语（现在分词）

**中文例句：**  
1. 系统运行稳定，支持高并发请求并自动扩展资源。  
2. 她站在窗边，望着远方的山峦，思考着下一步计划。  
3. 该算法高效处理大数据，显著降低计算时间。  
4. 他每天跑步，保持身体健康，同时缓解工作压力。  
5. 这家工厂采用自动化生产线，提高产能并减少人工错误。

> **English:**  
> 1. The system runs stably, supporting high-concurrency requests and automatically scaling resources.  
> 2. She stood by the window, gazing at the distant mountains, contemplating her next move.  
> 3. This algorithm processes big data efficiently, significantly reducing computation time.  
> 4. He runs every day, maintaining physical fitness while relieving work stress.  
> 5. This factory adopts automated production lines, boosting output and reducing human errors.

---

### 5. 同位语结构

**中文例句：**  
1. 深度学习，一种基于神经网络的机器学习方法，在图像识别领域表现出色。  
2. 北京，中国的首都，拥有超过三千年历史。  
3. 《三体》，一部著名的科幻小说，获得了雨果奖。  
4. 以太坊，一个去中心化的区块链平台，支持智能合约功能。  
5. 我的导师，张教授，在人工智能伦理方面有深入研究。

> **English:**  
> 1. Deep learning, a machine learning approach based on neural networks, performs remarkably well in image recognition.  
> 2. Beijing, the capital of China, has a history of over 3,000 years.  
> 3. *The Three-Body Problem*, a renowned science fiction novel, won the Hugo Award.  
> 4. Ethereum, a decentralized blockchain platform, supports smart contract functionality.  
> 5. My advisor, Professor Zhang, has done extensive research in AI ethics.
