### 其中
**among which** cache-aside is widely adopted due to its simplicity of implementation.

### 已经
后面是形容词用already，动词用has('s)/have('ve) + (被动been)

### 一直+动词
1. keep: 强调动作。
2. have been: 强调一段时间内一直在做
3. have always 过去分词: 强调习惯

### 一对一映射
1. 即可: just
2. 千万: never
3. 搞混: confuse A with B
4. 偏: is more
5. 从未/未曾: have never + 过去分词
6. 本可以: could have + 过去分词
7. 本该/本不该: should/shouldn't  have + 过去分词
8. 意味着: meaning/which means
9. 要/即将: be about to get/lose
10. 都肯定/否定: All (of)/None (of)
11. 都肯定/否定(两者): Both (of)/Neither (of)
12. 设法: manage to
13. ...就有了/没了: will come / be gone
14. 无论...还是...: **Whether** the result is good or bad
15. ....很久/不久之后: long/shortly after
16. 绕: convoluted/tricky
17. 反而: instead
18. 总有: there be always
19. 早就/早已: have long/already 过去分词
20. ...几乎没有...: have almost no
21. 最符合: best meet
22. 再(否定): any longer/more
